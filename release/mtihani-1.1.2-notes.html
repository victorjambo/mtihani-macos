Mtihani 1.1.2

This release refines the menu-bar experience for clearer, more consistent navigation.

Menu bar improvements

  • Menu actions now provide native macOS-style selection feedback when you hover over them.
  • The signed-out Sign in action now matches the appearance of the other menu actions.
  • The account submenu row now spans the full menu width, with its arrow aligned to the trailing edge.
  • Improved dividers and spacing around account and Screen Recording permission controls.
